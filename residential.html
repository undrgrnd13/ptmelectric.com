Residential services

Custom Builds

Full electrical for custom homes from the ground up. We wire the whole house, from service entrance to fixtures, as part of your build. Send your plans with a bid request and we’ll price the electrical scope.

Barns & Stables

Safe power for equestrian and agricultural buildings. Equestrian and agricultural buildings need power that holds up to how they’re used. PTM Electric is based in Loxahatchee.

Troubleshooting & Repair

Diagnose and fix faults quickly and correctly. If something isn’t working right, we find the cause before fixing it. Call 561-329-6974 to describe the problem.

Rewiring

Rewiring of faulty or outdated systems. Faulty or outdated wiring can be replaced so your home’s system is safe and up to code.

Generators

Standby generator installation, maintenance, and repair through our Stormpower Generators division. See generator services.

Panel & Service Upgrades

Electrical panel and service upgrades for modern loads. Many homes now run more than their panel was built for. We upgrade panels and electrical service to handle today’s loads.

FPL Overhead to Underground

Conversions from overhead to underground service. We convert overhead FPL service lines to underground service and coordinate with FPL on the conversion.

EV Chargers

Home charging for electric vehicles. We install EV chargers at your home, including the wiring and circuit the charger needs. Tell us your vehicle and where you’d like the charger in your bid request.

Surge Protection

Protection for your home’s electrical system. We install surge protection for homes. Ask about it with a panel upgrade or on its own.

Pool Electrical

Electrical work for pools. We handle the electrical work for residential pools. Include the pool details in your bid request so we can price the job.
